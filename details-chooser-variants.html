Details Chooser — Variants Archive

DD-130 prototype. Three variants explored for the Details mode chooser after Russell's feedback: "separate personalization from delivery, don't show it by default." Variant 3 went into production. The other two are kept here for reference in case we want to swap later.

Variant 1 · Pill toggle, no default + heading

Closest to original. Adds a heading + removes the default selection so content gates on click. Most compact (~60px). Risk: easy to miss visually.

Variant 2 · Inline buttons (text CTAs, no radio/cards)

Two plain buttons. Compact pre-state (~70px), very compact post-state (~30px). Visually distinct from shipping radio cards. Risk: buttons read as commands more than as a mode switcher.
